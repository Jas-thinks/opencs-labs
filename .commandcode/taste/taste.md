# Taste

## Workflow & Safety

- Wants to be asked before destructive actions on things outside the repo (e.g. killing an unrelated process holding a needed port) rather than having them done silently, and wants the exact PID/command shown before deciding. Confidence: 0.6
- Prefers investigate-and-report-a-plan-first workflows: do read-only research, write up the plan (options, files touched, risks, rollback), then stop and wait for explicit approval before changing anything. Confidence: 0.6
- On design/architecture work, wants to be interviewed with targeted clarifying questions (stack, scale, storage, sync, auth) before any plan is drafted — explicitly asks not to produce the final plan on the first turn. Confidence: 0.7
